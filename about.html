Shree Ganesh Engineering was built on one belief — precision manufacturing should be a launchpad for industrial success, not a bottleneck. Established in 2008 in Pune, Maharashtra, under the management of Maruti Shastri, we engineer scalable, sanitary, and high-integrity SS milk pumps, dairy valves, manhole covers, and vacuum components for businesses everywhere.

Our Quality Commitment

Shree Ganesh Engineering is highly committed to sourcing top-tier food-grade and chemical-grade stainless steel raw materials with traceable test certificates. We recognize that elements like dairy pump impellers and vessel manholes operate in sensitive, high-sanitation environments. Therefore, we ensure all weld zones are completely passivated and mirror-polished to prevent bacterial growth and maximize chemical corrosion resistance.
